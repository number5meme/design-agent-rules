# Presentation Workflow

Read by task: [Workflow](workflow.md) · [Method](design.md) · [Acceptance](quality.md). For local edits, read only the affected sections.

For creating, editing, and replicating presentations. Determine delivery per [Task Scope](../../standards/task-scope.md); for design, see [Presentation Design](design.md); check the finished deck per [Presentation Acceptance](quality.md).

## Inputs and Scope

Identify audience, communication goal, live talk or standalone reading use, aspect ratio, content basis, and editability requirements. Preserve the slide count and per-slide outline when they have been specified; when the slide count is not set, arrange by content and duration, confirming only when it affects scope or conflicts with content completeness. Include speaker notes, animation, and extra delivery formats as needed.

For topic input, first organize the arguments and required evidence; for a complete document, distill the structure and keep key qualifications; for a per-slide outline, keep the confirmed order and coverage. When facts are missing, mark the gap; do not pad or fabricate data to fill slides; confirm first when new research is needed and would expand scope.

## Production Paths

- New: clarify each slide's reader task, core message, and main chart or image, then decide the slide arrangement. Produce representative content slides and complex slides first, and expand after validating hierarchy and density; handle small edits directly.
- Edit: first check the source file, target slides, and related layouts, keeping content, notes, links, and animations not requested to change. Keep the original before format conversion; after conversion, check content, charts, fonts, and editable objects, and choose a fix or another route when losses are found.
- Replicate: rebuild per the reference's aspect ratio, positions, proportions, fonts, and hierarchy; zoom into regions when details are unclear. When editability is needed, rebuild text, charts, and necessary shapes as corresponding objects; assets such as photos may stay as images.

When using a template or reference, extract applicable layouts such as content and chart slides and shared elements, not just imitate the cover. Brand, copy, and data in the reference do not automatically become this deck's content. Handle style feedback per [Style Adjustment](../../standards/workflow.md#style-adjustment).

## Tools and Delivery

Check per [1. Intake and Clarification](../../standards/workflow.md#1-intake-and-clarification) whether the current environment can create, edit, render, and export the required format; validate key capabilities such as complex charts, fonts, or animation with representative slides first, then produce in batch. When using an intermediate format, check round-trip conversion losses; do not treat tool documentation as proof that it is installed or available.

Deliver the final file as agreed; when a project source is agreed, also provide the slides and assets it references, ensuring it still opens after moving directories. Recheck after the final export; record unavailable export or rendering capabilities as gaps, handle them per the general rules, and do not silently replace the delivery format.
