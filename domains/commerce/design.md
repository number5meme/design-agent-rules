# Product Detail Visuals

Read by task: [Workflow](workflow.md) · [Method](design.md) · [Acceptance](quality.md). For local edits, read only the affected sections.

## Content Organization

Identify the problems buyers need solved from real product information, then organize "concern → claim → evidence". Choose value explanations, structural details, usage scenarios, specs, compatibility, installation, or care content as the product needs; do not fix the number or order of modules.

Each module carries a clear information task. Merge repeated selling points; do not fabricate unsupported performance, materials, specs, or effects; comparisons use verifiable conditions, and clearly distinguish concept demonstrations from what the actual product looks like and how it performs.

## Product and Assets

Check shape, color, material, markings, and visible text against actual product images and confirmed information; structures not shown in images cannot be accepted as fact based on generated results. For concept products, first clarify their nature and baseline; do not call generated mockups real photos.

Arrange overall, structural, detail, and usage-scenario content by information need. Asset roles correspond to copy; detail descriptions should show the described detail, and size descriptions use real data. Choose shooting angle, models, flat lay, or compositing by display purpose; do not force full-body models or regenerating every item.

When the style is rejected, redo the image-text expression per [Style Adjustment](../../standards/workflow.md#style-adjustment); product facts and fidelity requirements remain valid.

## Module Organization

Keep consistent typography, color, annotation language, and spacing rhythm; adapt module height, aspect ratio, and layout to the carrier and content. Set titles and supporting notes by information volume; do not force three text levels in every module.
