# Knowledge and Standards Governance

Use this guide to decide when a project finding has enough evidence to become a shared rule.

## Recording and Promotion
1. For work requiring saved records, keep project feedback in the existing review record or the corresponding section of a combined record, with the version, specific problem, change, and result. Follow [Record Ownership](../standards/task-scope.md#record-ownership); review-only findings stay in the reply unless saving was requested.
2. When it has reuse value, distill a de-projectized method in the project record with initial status "candidate", keeping applicable scope, counterexamples, and verification status. Specific evidence, verbatim quotes, versions, and source mappings stay in the project; general files do not link to projects or tasks.
3. Mark it "verified" when it proves effective again in another independent scenario, or when it has a clear, reliable basis and its applicability has been checked. Repeated changes within one project do not count as independent verification.
4. To become a default rule, write the rule and its applicable boundaries into the corresponding standard; keep source, approval, and replacement history in local maintenance records, not in general execution standards.
5. When new evidence conflicts, mark it "pending review" or "deprecated", keeping the old record and reason so history is not forgotten.

Preferences the user states explicitly can be recorded locally or in the project as "user preference" without waiting for multi-project verification, but are not distributed with the general agent. Confirmed general methods are updated through the process above; personal accounts, authorizations, and project aesthetics cannot be promoted along with them.

## When to Update
Capture records when direction choices, major changes, or deliveries produce reusable findings. When a new project hits a corresponding problem, look up verified conclusions and related counterexamples; do not traverse all knowledge or other projects by default. Add a finding only when you have evidence for it.

## Save Design Judgment, Not Just Style
Projects should keep verbatim feedback, product/scenario interpretation, actions taken, results, and counterexamples. Distinguish: user preference (explicit source), category hypothesis (to be verified), and measured conclusion (with evidence). Colors, materials, and layouts chosen by a project are not directly promoted to general defaults.
