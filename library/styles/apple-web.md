# Apple Web Style Reference

For product sites, hardware showcases, brand features, and lightweight stores. The style is formed by clear hierarchy, content-led composition, refined typesetting, and direct feedback; app interfaces decide density and navigation by their own tasks.

## Choosing a Direction

When only an "Apple style" is requested, autonomously decide the layout, color, type hierarchy, and asset approach suited to the project, without automatically adopting a specific parameter preset. For specific parameters, see [When to Read a Preset](../presets/index.md#when-to-read-a-preset).

| Dimension | Basis and optional expression |
|---|---|
| Layout | Choose centered showcase, split columns, editorial arrangement, asymmetric composition, or comparison grid by narrative and operating task; do not fix the first-screen structure |
| Color | Choose neutral, warm, colorful, or dark surfaces by brand, product, and mood; the action color can be decided independently and is not limited to blue |
| Page rhythm | Decide continuous same-color, local emphasis, or light/dark switching by information relationships; alternating sections are not required |
| Assets | Photography, interface, illustration, or text can carry the visual subject, decided by content and available assets |
| Material and shape | Choose rounded or square corners, translucent or solid surfaces by purpose; use glass, gradients, and shadows only when expression needs them |

The above are optional directions, not an exhaustive list of layouts. Keep a clear reading order, recognizable actions, and consistent project semantics; composition can vary with content across pages.

## Typesetting and Assets

- Check typeface, weight, line height, and letter spacing by role and real language combination; choose a Display/Text split or other suitable typefaces, and do not mechanically inherit negative heading letter spacing.
- Verify color and states per [Color Pairing](../../standards/defaults.md#color-pairing).
- Image proportions, text width, grid, and whitespace are decided by content and frame. Check subject cropping on mobile, and reserve image space to avoid layout jumps on load.
- Check floating surfaces for readability over dynamic backgrounds; gradients, materials, and decoration can serve brand mood and distinctiveness without obscuring information or actions.

## Components and Verification

Choose navigation structure and necessary entry points by the current page; buttons, options, and search use the project's agreed semantics and states. Verify control sizes, hit areas, safe areas, and fixed bar spacing per device; do not infer native capabilities from appearance parameters.

After choosing it, render representative pages and verify the rules, states, and content effects the project actually adopts per the [Style Consistency Review](../references/style-consistency-review.md). Fonts and images use resources available to the project; supplement undefined business states per the task.
